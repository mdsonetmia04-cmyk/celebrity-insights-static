Grammy nominations 2027: RAYE leads with 11, Rosalía earns nine. Full list, Album of the Year race, snubs, and ceremony details.

Advertisement
Advertisement

The 2027 Grammy nominations are out, and RAYE leads the field with 11, announced on Monday, November 16 — including Record, Album and Song of the Year.

Rosalía follows with nine for Lux (Complete Works), days after her history-making Latin Grammy night. Phoebe Bridgers, Miranda Lambert, Ella Langley and Olivia Rodrigo each earned seven, while Olivia Dean, Bruno Mars and Leon Thomas picked up six apiece. Taylor Swift, Geese and Kehlani have five each.

An Eight-Way Race for Album of the Year

Album of the Year is an eight-way race: Bridgers (Lost Weekend), Dean (The Art of Loving), Langley (Dandelion), Mars (The Romantic), RAYE (This Music May Contain Hope), Rodrigo (You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love), Rosalía (Lux (Complete Works)) and Swift (The Life of a Showgirl).

Advertisement

Record of the Year includes RAYE's "Where Is My Husband!", Rosalía's "Berghain" with Björk and Yves Tumor, Swift's "The Fate of Ophelia" and Langley's "Choosin' Texas" — the song that recently set the record for the most weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100.

Milestones, Newcomers and Snubs

Beyoncé reached 100 career Grammy nominations with a Best R&B Solo Performance nod. Best New Artist includes Geese, Ella Langley, Don Toliver, Remi Wolf and Slayyyter, while a new Best Asian Pop Music Performance category puts SB19, BINI, LiSA, IVE, KiiiKiii and BABYMETAL with Polyphia in contention.

Advertisement

Harry Styles, Justin Bieber, Ed Sheeran, Doja Cat and Lizzo were among the notable names shut out. The 69th Grammy Awards air on Sunday, February 7, 2027, from Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles on ABC, Disney+ and Hulu, with voting running December 10 to January 7. The host is yet to be announced.

Quick Answers

Who leads the 2027 Grammy nominations? RAYE, with 11 nominations, announced on November 16.

When are the 2027 Grammy Awards? Sunday, February 7, 2027, from Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, airing on ABC, Disney+ and Hulu.

How many nominations does Rosalía have? Nine, for Lux (Complete Works).

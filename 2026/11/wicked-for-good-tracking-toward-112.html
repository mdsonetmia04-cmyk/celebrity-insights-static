Wicked: For Good Tracking Toward a $112 Million-Plus Opening

Hollywood
Nov 17, 2026
2 min read

Wicked: For Good is tracking toward a $112M-plus opening on Nov 21, 2026. Forecasts, budget, and the sequel's box office outlook.

Advertisement
Advertisement
Advertisement

Wicked: For Good opens in US cinemas on Friday, November 21, 2026 — and the tracking says the sequel could open even bigger than the first film.

Deadline's tracking puts the domestic opening at $112–115 million. Box Office Theory goes further, forecasting up to $155 million-plus, with a global opening near $200 million. Universal's own projection is more conservative, at around $125 million. Tracking is a forecast, not a result — but every forecaster is pointing in the same direction: up.

Beating the First Film's Opening

The first Wicked opened to $112.5 million in November 2024, on its way to $473.2 million domestic and $756.4 million worldwide. If the sequel lands at the top of its tracking range, it will clear that opening weekend — a rare feat for a second part, and a sign that the first film's audience is returning.

Advertisement

The sequel carries a reported budget of around $150 million, so a big opening matters: it needs to perform like an event film, not just a follow-up.

Advertisement

The Story Continues

Cynthia Erivo returns as Elphaba and Ariana Grande as Glinda, with Jon M. Chu directing again. The second film picks up where the first left off, carrying the story through to its conclusion — the part of the tale the first film deliberately held back.

There is no streaming date yet. For now, the only way to see it is in cinemas, starting November 21. The tracking will be tested against reality that weekend — and if the forecasts hold, Wicked: For Good will be the biggest opening of the month.

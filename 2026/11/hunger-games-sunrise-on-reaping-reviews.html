Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping Reviews Are Split as Early Screenings Start Tonight

Hollywood
Nov 18, 2026
3 min read

Sunrise on the Reaping reviews are split — 75% on Rotten Tomatoes — as early screenings start tonight. Release date, cast, and box office tracking.

The first reviews for The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping are in, and critics are genuinely divided. The review embargo lifted on Monday, November 17, and the film's Rotten Tomatoes score has shifted several times since — opening at 70%, dipping to 69%, and sitting at 75% from 80 reviews as of Wednesday. Metacritic tells a cooler story, with a 56 from 27 reviews.

Advertisement

The timing could hardly be tighter: early-access screenings begin tonight, Wednesday, November 18, at 6 p.m. local time in premium formats only, two days before the wide release on Friday, November 20. The film runs 158 minutes, the longest in the franchise.

A Divided Critical Response

The praise has centred on the cast. Joseph Zada, playing 16-year-old Haymitch Abernathy, has drawn repeated praise for the performance's emotional weight, alongside Mckenna Grace and Jesse Plemons, while several critics called the film a better prequel than The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes for its tighter pacing and clearer tone.

Advertisement

The detractors have been blunt. The Guardian gave the film one star, The Times gave it two, and The New York Times called it "both bloated and limited." Variety described it as meandering and thin, though directed by Francis Lawrence in what it called "Hunger Games Classic" style. The story returns to Panem 24 years before the original film, to the morning of the reaping for the 50th Hunger Games — the Second Quarter Quell, in which the Capitol doubles the tribute count to 48.

Advertisement

Tonight's Screenings and the $100 Million Question

Tonight's early-access shows play only in premium formats: IMAX, Dolby Cinema, 4DX, ScreenX, Cinemark XD, Regal RPX and AMC Prime. Forecasts put the domestic opening at $80–100 million (Box Office Pro), with Box Office Theory forecasting $105 million or more, a $116 million pinpoint and a $140 million high end. All are forecasts, not results.

The optimism rests on presales: Deadline reported advance sales running ahead of both Wicked: For Good and Michael at the same stage, the best Lionsgate has recorded since the pandemic. The last entry, Songbirds & Snakes, opened to $44.6 million in 2023, while the franchise has grossed more than $3.3 billion worldwide.

Quick Answers

What is the Rotten Tomatoes score for Sunrise on the Reaping? 75% from 80 reviews as of Wednesday, though the score has moved several times since the embargo lifted; Metacritic sits at 56.

When do the early screenings start? Wednesday, November 18, at 6 p.m. local time, in premium formats only. The wide release follows on Friday, November 20.

How big is the opening expected to be? Forecasts put it at $80–100 million domestically, with some trackers going above $105 million.

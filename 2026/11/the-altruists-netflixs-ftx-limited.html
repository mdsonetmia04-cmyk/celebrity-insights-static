Netflix's The Altruists arrives Nov 19, 2026 — an 8-episode FTX limited series with Julia Garner and Anthony Boyle. What to expect.

Netflix's The Altruists arrives on Thursday, November 19, 2026 — an eight-episode limited series dramatising the rise and collapse of crypto exchange FTX, one of the biggest financial scandals of the decade.

Julia Garner plays Caroline Ellison, the former Alameda Research chief, and Anthony Boyle plays Sam Bankman-Fried, the FTX founder whose empire collapsed in a matter of days in November 2022. It is the real-life fraud story behind years of headlines, told as prestige drama.

Advertisement

Why This Story, Why Now

The FTX collapse is still recent enough to feel current: billions of dollars of customer money gone, a trial that ended in conviction, and a crypto industry that is still living with the consequences. The series arrives three years after the collapse, at a moment when the story has had time to become a complete narrative — beginning, middle, and end — rather than breaking news.

Advertisement

What to Expect

Eight episodes, released together, following the two central figures from the inside of the empire to its fall. Garner and Boyle carry the series, and the draw is the same one that made the real story so widely followed: how something that looked like the future of finance turned out to be built on nothing.

It is the week's most talked-about new series — and the rare drama where the ending is already public record, and the question is how it got there.

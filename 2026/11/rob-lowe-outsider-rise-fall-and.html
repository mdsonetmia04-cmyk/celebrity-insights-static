Rob Lowe: The Outsider — The Rise, Fall, and Reinvention Story Coming to Netflix

Hollywood
Nov 15, 2026
2 min read

Rob Lowe: The Outsider premieres on Netflix Nov 24, 2026. The documentary traces Lowe's rise, scandal, recovery, and second act.

Rob Lowe is telling his own story in Rob Lowe: The Outsider, a new Netflix documentary that premieres on Tuesday, November 24, 2026. Directed by Chris Smith — the filmmaker behind Tiger King, Fyre, and Sr. — the film traces Lowe's career from teenage stardom through public scandal and recovery to a second act on television, built from new interviews and archival footage. Netflix released the first trailer on November 3.

The Rise: The Outsiders to the Brat Pack

The story opens, for most movie fans, in 1983, with Francis Ford Coppola's The Outsiders. Lowe was 19, part of an ensemble that became the defining young cast of the decade, and the film made him famous fast. St. Elmo's Fire followed, and with it the "Brat Pack" label that stuck to Lowe and his co-stars — a tag he has spent much of his career both living with and pushing against.

The documentary revisits that rise through the people who were there, including interviews with Lowe's wife of more than 30 years, Sheryl Berkoff, and former co-stars Demi Moore and Gwyneth Paltrow.

The Fall, and the Reinvention

The film does not look away from the low point. The trailer confirms the documentary addresses Lowe's 1988 sex-tape scandal, his struggles with addiction, and his recovery — the stretch of his life that nearly ended the career the Brat Pack years had built.

What followed is the second half of the story, and the reason the documentary is called The Outsider. Lowe rebuilt himself on television: first as Sam Seaborn on The West Wing, then as the relentlessly optimistic Chris Traeger on Parks and Recreation, a role that introduced him to a new generation. The documentary follows that arc in full for the first time, pairing the interviews with a career-spanning look back at the moments — public and private — that shaped him.

What to Expect, and When

Rob Lowe: The Outsider premieres globally on Netflix on November 24, 2026. It is executive produced by Marc Gurvitz, Jon Liebman, and Zoe Rogovin.

The documentary arrives alongside a second piece of Lowe news: from Thursday, December 3, new video episodes of his podcast Literally! With Rob Lowe will premiere only on Netflix, one every Thursday, while the audio stays on the SiriusXM Podcast Network. Netflix will also carry more than 30 episodes from the podcast's back catalog.

For viewers who know Lowe only from one era — the Brat Pack heartthrob, the West Wing staffer, or the Parks and Rec optimist — the documentary is built to connect the three, and to show the work in between.

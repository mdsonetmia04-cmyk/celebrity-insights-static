Ramayana Nears ₹1,500 Crore Worldwide in 14 Days — and Tops IMDb's Global Chart

Bollywood
Nov 18, 2026
2 min read

Ramayana nears ₹1,500 crore worldwide in 14 days and ranks No. 3 on IMDb's global chart. Trade figures, records, and the epic's box office run.

Ramayana: Part 1 is on the verge of the ₹1,500 crore club. The Nitesh Tiwari epic reached ₹1,483.50 crore worldwide in 13 days, according to industry tracker Sacnilk, and Day 14 live estimates put it within touching distance of the milestone as Wednesday's shows close. All box-office figures are trade estimates.

Advertisement

Day 14 is also closing in on another landmark: live estimates put the India gross at ₹998.60 crore, just short of ₹1,000 crore from India alone. The film stars Ranbir Kapoor, Sai Pallavi and Yash, and opened on November 5.

The Numbers So Far

Day 13 (Tuesday, November 17) brought in ₹28.70 crore net in India across 22,551 shows, taking the India net total to ₹821.30 crore and the India gross to ₹981.50 crore. Overseas markets have added an estimated ₹502 crore, about a third of the worldwide total.

Advertisement

Audiences are rating it highly too: the film holds a 77% critics' score and a 94% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes. The Day 14 final figure, including overseas earnings, arrives later tonight — and with it, the likely confirmation of the ₹1,500 crore mark.

Advertisement

No. 3 on IMDb Worldwide

Beyond the box office, Ramayana has reached No. 3 on IMDb's list of the 10 most popular movies worldwide this week, dated November 17 — the only Indian film in the global top 10. It sits behind You Can See Everything and Ebenezer, and ahead of Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey at No. 4.

Advertisement

IMDb's popularity chart is based on user interest and page views rather than ratings, so the ranking reflects how much attention the film is drawing worldwide. The film carries an 8.2 user rating on the platform from around 40,000 ratings.

Quick Answers

How much has Ramayana earned worldwide? ₹1,483.50 crore in 13 days per Sacnilk, with Day 14 estimates putting the ₹1,500 crore mark within reach. All figures are trade estimates.

How much has Ramayana earned in India? ₹821.30 crore net and ₹981.50 crore gross after 13 days, with the India gross nearing ₹1,000 crore on Day 14.

Where does Ramayana rank on IMDb? No. 3 on IMDb's most popular movies worldwide this week, the only Indian film in the global top 10.

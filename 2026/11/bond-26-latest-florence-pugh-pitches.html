Bond 26 Latest: Florence Pugh Pitches Herself as the Villain as the Royal Family Welcomes Pattinson

Hollywood
Nov 17, 2026
2 min read

Florence Pugh pitched herself as the Bond 26 villain, and the Royal Family welcomed Pattinson. The latest on the new 007 era.

Advertisement
Advertisement

Days after Amazon MGM Studios announced Robert Pattinson as the new 007, Florence Pugh has pitched herself to play the villain of Bond 26 — by texting director Denis Villeneuve directly.

Pugh revealed the pitch at ELLE's Women in Hollywood event on November 16, saying she reached out to Villeneuve as soon as the casting was announced. She has already worked with both Pattinson and Villeneuve on Dune: Part Three, which opens in cinemas on December 18. Nothing is confirmed — no villain, M, Q or love interest is cast yet — but the pitch shows how fast the new Bond era has become Hollywood's most wanted ticket.

A Royal Welcome — and a Reported Three-Film Deal

The casting has also drawn a royal response: the British Royal Family's official account welcomed him with the message "Welcome to His Majesty's Secret Service, Mr Bond" — Pattinson is the first actor to play 007 during the reign of King Charles III.

Advertisement

Reports say Pattinson has signed on for three films, and that he beat a shortlist reported to include Nicholas Hoult, Jack Lowden, Josh O'Connor and Callum Turner. The announcement itself gave no title or release date, though production is widely expected to begin in 2027 ahead of a rumoured 2028 release.

What Is Confirmed So Far

What is certain: Villeneuve directs from a script by Steven Knight, with Amy Pascal and David Heyman producing. Pattinson, 40, becomes the seventh actor to play Bond in the official film series, succeeding Daniel Craig, whose run ended with No Time to Die in 2021. The casting was announced on November 16, ending years of speculation — and the conversation about who joins him is only getting started.

Quick Answers

Who is the new James Bond? Robert Pattinson, announced by Amazon MGM Studios on November 16, 2026.

Who directs Bond 26? Denis Villeneuve, from a script by Steven Knight.

Is Florence Pugh the Bond villain? No — nothing is confirmed; Pugh has only pitched herself for the role by texting Villeneuve.

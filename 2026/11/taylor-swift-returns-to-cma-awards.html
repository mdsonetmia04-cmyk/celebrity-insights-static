Taylor Swift Returns to the CMA Awards Stage After 13 Years

Music
Nov 15, 2026
3 min read

Taylor Swift returns to the CMA Awards stage after 13 years. Performance details for the 60th CMA Awards on Nov 18, 2026.

Taylor Swift will perform at the 60th Annual CMA Awards on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, her first performance at the country music ceremony in 13 years. The Country Music Association and ABC announced the news on Friday, November 13, with Good Morning America confirming the booking the same morning — just five days before the ceremony. Swift had previously confirmed she would attend the show, but her performance had not been announced until Friday, making the booking the final-week headline of a milestone show. The Taylor Swift CMA Awards 2026 appearance comes as the ceremony turns 60 this year.

The 13-Year Gap

Swift last performed at the CMA Awards on November 6, 2013, when she sang an acoustic version of "Red" alongside country legends Alison Krauss and Vince Gill at Nashville's Bridgestone Arena. In the years since, she moved from country to pop and became the biggest touring act in the world, while her CMA appearances became rare. Her return to the same stage — the same arena, in the city where her career began — is the story of the night before a single note is sung.

She does not return as a nostalgia act or a surprise tribute guest. She returns as a nominee, in a competitive category, on a night built around the genre's present as much as its history.

The Nomination: A Song From Toy Story 5

Swift is nominated for Single of the Year for "I Knew It, I Knew You," the original song she wrote for Disney and Pixar's Toy Story 5, produced by Swift and Jack Antonoff. It is her 26th career CMA nomination and her first at the show since 2022.

In the Single of the Year category, she faces two singles from Ella Langley — "Be Her" and "Choosin' Texas" — along with Lainey Wilson's "Somewhere Over Laredo" and Cody Johnson's "The Fall." A win would be a notable one: a song written for an animated film taking a major country award.

What Will She Perform?

As of this writing, the song Swift will sing has not been officially announced. The CMA and ABC announcement named no song for her performance, and no collaborators have been confirmed. Parade reported that she is expected to perform the nominated song, "I Knew It, I Knew You," but that remains unconfirmed by the organizers — worth stating plainly, because award-show speculation has a habit of running ahead of the facts. The Taylor Swift CMA performance setlist, in other words, is still a surprise.

Advertisement

The 60th CMA Awards: Host, Lineup, and Tributes

Lainey Wilson hosts the ceremony for the third consecutive year, at the Bridgestone Arena in Nashville. Swift joins a performance lineup that includes Miranda Lambert, Kacey Musgraves, Tim McGraw, Cody Johnson, Ella Langley, Zach Top, Tucker Wetmore, Luke Combs, Megan Moroney, Vince Gill, and The Red Clay Strays.

The night also includes a tribute to Dolly Parton, and a performance honoring Brooks & Dunn, this year's recipients of the CMA Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award, featuring Brothers Osborne, HARDY, Luke Bryan, Marcus King, and Halestorm. Swift has not been placed in either tribute; her announced role is her own performance.

How to Watch

The 60th Annual CMA Awards air live on Wednesday, November 18, from 8:00 to 11:00 p.m. ET on ABC and Disney+, and will be available to stream the following day on Hulu. For viewers outside the United States, check local listings — the show's international availability varies by territory.

Thirteen years is a long time between performances. On Wednesday night, the stage that helped launch her career gets her back — as a nominee, a performer, and the night's biggest headline.

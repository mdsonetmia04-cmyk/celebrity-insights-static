Madden: Nicolas Cage Becomes John Madden in the Week's Most Talked-About Streaming Premiere

Hollywood
Nov 16, 2026
3 min read

Madden premieres on Prime Video Nov 18, 2026. Nicolas Cage plays John Madden in David O. Russell's biopic — cast and streaming details.

One of the most searched movies of the week arrives with almost no fanfare. Madden, David O. Russell's biopic of football coach, broadcaster and video-game namesake John Madden, premieres globally on Prime Video on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, with Nicolas Cage in the title role and Christian Bale as Raiders owner Al Davis. The film skips a wide theatrical release, opening instead with a limited theatrical run — a release strategy that has become almost as talked-about as the casting.

Advertisement

Cage, Bale, and a Famous Transformation

Cage plays Madden across the defining chapters of his life: the Oakland Raiders head coach who went 103-32-7 from 1969 to 1978, never had a losing season, and won Super Bowl XI against the Minnesota Vikings on January 9, 1977; the broadcaster who became the most famous voice in American football; and the namesake of the Madden NFL video game series, which still carries his name. To play him, the 62-year-old Cage wears a shaggy wig, prosthetics and padding, a transformation that dominated reaction to the trailer Amazon MGM released in September. The trailer has drawn more than 8.6 million views.

Advertisement

Bale, a regular Russell collaborator, plays Al Davis, the Raiders owner whose partnership with Madden is the film's central relationship. Kathryn Hahn plays Madden's wife Virginia, Sienna Miller plays Davis's wife Carol, and the ensemble includes John Mulaney and Shane Gillis. Russell directed from a screenplay he wrote with Cambron Clark. It is Russell's first film since Amsterdam (2022), and reports have put the production budget at around $75 million. The film is rated R and runs 1 hour 50 minutes. Madden himself died in 2021.

Advertisement

A Quiet Release for a Big Film

Amazon MGM announced in August 2026 that Madden would forgo a nationwide theatrical run in favour of the Prime Video premiere. A limited theatrical run opened on November 11 for awards qualification, ahead of the global streaming premiere on November 18, and marketing has stayed muted — trade coverage has described the rollout as a "muted thud" and noted that the trailer's view count owes more to Madden and the NFL than to the film itself.

Advertisement

The first verdicts arrive this week. The critics' screening takes place on Monday, November 16, with the review embargo lifting about 15 hours later, so the first reviews should land on Tuesday, November 17 — one day before the film streams. That leaves audiences with very little time between the reviews and the premiere itself.

Advertisement

Why It Matters

Sports biopics live or die on whether the central performance feels like the person, and Madden is asking Cage — one of the most imitated actors alive — to disappear into one of the most imitated voices in sports. Whether the film works as drama or becomes a curiosity is the question the reviews will answer first. Either way, it is the highest-profile new film streaming this week, and the search numbers suggest plenty of people intend to find out for themselves.

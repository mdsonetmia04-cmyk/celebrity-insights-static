The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping — Release Date, Cast, and the 3D Plan

Hollywood
Nov 15, 2026
4 min read

Sunrise on the Reaping opens Nov 20, 2026 in theaters and IMAX. Release date, cast, story, and the 3D plan for the new Hunger Games prequel.

The Hunger Games returns to cinemas this week. The Sunrise on the Reaping release date is Friday, November 20, 2026, when the prequel opens in theaters and IMAX in the United States. It is the sixth film in the franchise and the second prequel, following The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes (2023). This time, the story goes back to the Games that made Haymitch Abernathy a victor — and the film will also be the first in the series released in 3D.

The Story: The 50th Hunger Games

Sunrise on the Reaping adapts Suzanne Collins's novel of the same name, published in March 2025. The film is set 24 years before Katniss Everdeen first volunteers as tribute, during the 50th Hunger Games — the Second Quarter Quell. For a Quarter Quell, the Capitol doubles the number of tributes, so four young people are reaped from each district instead of two.

At the center of the story is a 16-year-old Haymitch Abernathy of District 12, the character Woody Harrelson played as an adult in the original trilogy. The novel follows Haymitch from the morning of the reaping through an arena designed to punish defiance, and through the victory that shapes the bitter, sharp-tongued mentor audiences already know. Francis Lawrence directs, continuing a run that covers every Hunger Games film since Catching Fire (2013).

The Cast: New Faces and Returning Ones

Joseph Zada, an Australian actor recently seen in Netflix's East of Eden, stars as the young Haymitch. Whitney Peak plays Lenore Dove Baird, Haymitch's girlfriend, and Mckenna Grace plays Maysilee Donner, a fellow District 12 tribute and the original owner of the mockingjay pin that later becomes Katniss's symbol.

The Capitol side of the cast is stacked. Ralph Fiennes plays President Coriolanus Snow, the third actor to take the role after Donald Sutherland in the original trilogy and Tom Blyth in The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes. Jesse Plemons plays a young Plutarch Heavensbee, seen here not yet as Head Gamemaker but as a Capitol cameraman filming propaganda. Elle Fanning plays Effie Trinket, the escort role originated by Elizabeth Banks, and Glenn Close plays Drusilla Sickle, the District 12 escort. Kieran Culkin appears as Caesar Flickerman, with Kelvin Harrison Jr. as Beetee, Lili Taylor as Mags, and Billy Porter as Magno Stift.

Original trilogy stars are also back. Jennifer Lawrence confirmed on October 28 that she returns as Katniss Everdeen, alongside Josh Hutcherson as Peeta Mellark and Harrelson as the older Haymitch. Lionsgate has not disclosed how large their roles are, and the film's central story remains Haymitch's.

The 3D Release and Meta's VR Glasses

The Hunger Games 3D release is the film's biggest technical talking point. On November 2, 2026, Lionsgate and Meta announced that Sunrise on the Reaping would open in 3D in theaters, with the conversion completed alongside director Francis Lawrence and DNEG, the Oscar-winning visual effects studio whose Stereo team has worked on 3D conversion for more than 15 years. It is the first time Meta has partnered with a Hollywood studio to convert a feature film into 3D for its theatrical release.

The plan does not end at the cinema. After its theatrical run, the film will also be available in 3D on Meta's VR Glasses, the device Meta unveiled at its Connect 2026 event in September. The glasses are due to go on sale in spring 2027, reportedly starting at $1,299. The two companies have said additional Lionsgate titles will follow on the device, with details to be announced later. Neither company has disclosed the financial terms of the deal.

Trailers, Premiere, and Box Office Tracking

The first teaser trailer dropped on November 20, 2025 — exactly one year before the film's release — and Lionsgate released a full-length trailer on September 16, 2026. The film held its London premiere on October 28, where attendees were allowed to share short first reactions online while full critics' reviews are held until closer to release.

Trade tracking points to a big opening. Deadline reported on November 6 that the film's North American opening floor had risen above $80 million, with advance ticket sales running ahead of recent comparables and described as Lionsgate's strongest post-Covid presale run. That is a trade estimate based on presales, not a result — the first hard numbers arrive with Thursday-night preview grosses on November 19–20.

For viewers catching up, all five earlier Hunger Games films arrived on Peacock in the US on November 14, 2026, six days before the new film opens. No streaming date for Sunrise on the Reaping itself has been announced; for now, it is a theaters-only release.

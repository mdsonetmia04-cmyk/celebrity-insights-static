Ramayana Crosses ₹1,400 Crore — Now Bollywood's No. 2 Worldwide Ever

Bollywood
Nov 17, 2026
2 min read

Ramayana crossed ₹1,400 crore worldwide, now Bollywood's No. 2 grosser ever. Trade figures, records, and Ranbir's ₹700 crore club.

Ramayana: Part 1 has crossed ₹1,400 crore worldwide, becoming the fastest Indian film ever to reach the mark — and overtaking Dhurandhar to become Bollywood's second-biggest worldwide grosser of all time.

Trade trackers differ slightly on the exact figure, as they usually do at this speed: Bollywood Hungama reports ₹1,400.06 crore worldwide, while Sacnilk puts the 12-day total at ₹1,437.25 crore. Either way, the milestone is the same — and the film got there in under two weeks.

Passing Dhurandhar

The film has now passed Dhurandhar (2025, ₹1,350.83 crore) to take the No. 2 spot among Bollywood films worldwide, excluding China. Only one film stands ahead of it: Dhurandhar: The Revenge, at ₹1,852.44 crore.

It is a follow-up, not a repeat, for this blog: we covered the film when it crossed ₹1,100 crore in nine days. The pace has not slowed since — the next ₹300 crore came in about three days.

Ranbir Kapoor Joins the ₹700 Crore Club

The domestic numbers are just as striking. The film's India nett collection stands at ₹755.99 crore, putting Ranbir Kapoor into the ₹700 crore club — only the third actor to get there, alongside Allu Arjun and Ranveer Singh. He is also the second-fastest actor to reach ₹700 crore in India, doing it in 11 days.

What Comes Next

The question now is how far the run goes. The gap to the all-time No. 1 is still more than ₹400 crore, and the film's daily collections will decide whether that record is in reach or out of it. What is already settled is the film's place in the record books: the fastest Indian film to ₹1,400 crore, and Bollywood's No. 2 worldwide grosser ever.

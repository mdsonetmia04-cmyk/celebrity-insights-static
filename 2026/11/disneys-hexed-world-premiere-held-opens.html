Disney's Hexed: World Premiere Held, Opens in Cinemas November 25

Hollywood
Nov 18, 2026
2 min read

Disney's Hexed premiered in Hollywood and opens Nov 25, 2026. Voice cast, story, first reactions, and the Thanksgiving release details.

Walt Disney Animation Studios held the world premiere of Hexed at the El Capitan Theatre in Hollywood on November 12, with Hailee Steinfeld, Rashida Jones and Jodie Foster leading the voice cast on the purple carpet. The film opens in US cinemas on Wednesday, November 25 — the day before Thanksgiving — and reaches UK cinemas on November 27.

Hexed is the studio's 65th animated feature and its first film that is not a sequel since Wish in 2023. It is directed by Jason Hand and Fawn Veerasunthorn, co-directed by Josie Trinidad, and produced by Roy Conli and Yvett Merino.

The Story and the Voice Cast

The film follows Billie Doe, an impulsive and unconventional teenager voiced by Steinfeld, who discovers secret magical abilities that carry her out of suburbia and into Hexe, a vast realm of witches. Rashida Jones voices her mother, Alice Doe, and Jodie Foster voices the villain, Queen Celeste.

The wider cast includes Walton Goggins as Beef Roger Crummchuk, a magical three-eyed cat, alongside Henry Winkler, Dan Stevens, Brett Gelman, Tracey Ullman and Stephen Fry. The screenplay comes from Disney Animation chief creative officer Jared Bush and Dana Ledoux Miller, with an original score by Kris Bowers.

First Reactions and the Thanksgiving Race

First reactions from the premiere crowd have been mostly positive, with particular praise for Foster's villain and for the mother-daughter story at the film's centre. These are premiere reactions rather than full reviews, which arrive closer to release.

The PG-rated film plays with a new short, Lilo & Scratch, in front of it, and tickets are already on sale. It opens into the Thanksgiving corridor, one of the year's biggest family-movie windows.

Quick Answers

When does Hexed come out? November 25, 2026, in US cinemas, and November 27 in the UK.

Who voices Billie in Hexed? Hailee Steinfeld, with Rashida Jones as her mother Alice and Jodie Foster as the villain Queen Celeste.

When was the Hexed premiere? November 12, 2026, at the El Capitan Theatre in Hollywood.

Rosalía Makes Latin Grammy History — First Woman to Win Album of the Year Three Times

Music
Nov 17, 2026
3 min read

Rosalía made Latin Grammy history, winning Album of the Year a third time — the first woman to do it. Full story from the 2026 ceremony.

Advertisement
Advertisement
Advertisement

Rosalía made history at the 27th Latin Grammy Awards on Thursday, November 12, 2026, winning Album of the Year for Lux (Complete Works) — her third win in the category, and the first time a woman has won it three times as a lead artist. The ceremony took place at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas, and Rosalía opened the show herself with a medley from the album before collecting the night's biggest prize.

Advertisement

A Third Album of the Year

The win puts Rosalía alongside Alejandro Sanz and Juanes, the only other artists with three Album of the Year victories; only Juan Luis Guerra, with four, has won it more often. Her previous wins came for El Mal Querer in 2019 and Motomami in 2022. Accepting the award from Juanes, she called Lux "a leap into the void" — after El Mal Querer felt like a dream and Motomami felt like a gamble.

Advertisement

It was not her only prize of the night. "La Perla," her collaboration with Yahritza y Su Esencia, won Song of the Year and Best Pop Song, and Lux also took Best Alternative Music Album, with "Sauvignon Blanc" winning Best Short Form Music Video — five trophies in total, making her the night's biggest winner.

Advertisement

What Lux Is

Lux, released in November 2025, is Rosalía's fourth studio album — an orchestral, classical-leaning record sung in 13 languages, a long way from the flamenco-pop of her breakthrough. It topped Billboard's Top Latin Albums chart, and its opening medley at the ceremony — "La Rumba Del Perdón," "La Perla" and "Dios Es Un Stalker," staged among dancers with halos on a cloud-like set — was the night's most talked-about performance.

Advertisement

The eligibility window for the 2026 awards covered recordings released between June 1, 2025 and May 31, 2026, and Rosalía beat a field that included Karol G, Anitta and Jorge Drexler for the top prize.

Advertisement

Why the Record Matters

The Latin Grammys have handed out Album of the Year for 27 years, and no woman had ever won it three times before — as a lead artist, Rosalía now stands alone. The win also caps a run in which each of her last three albums has taken the category, a consistency no other artist in the ceremony's history can match.

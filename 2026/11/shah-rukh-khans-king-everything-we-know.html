Shah Rukh Khan's King: Everything We Know About His Christmas Comeback

Bollywood
Nov 16, 2026
2 min read

Shah Rukh Khan's King releases Dec 24, 2026. Everything we know about SRK's Christmas comeback with Siddharth Anand and Suhana Khan.

Advertisement

Shah Rukh Khan returns to theatres on Thursday, December 24, 2026, with King — his first theatrical release since Dunki in 2023, and his second film with Pathaan director Siddharth Anand. The action thriller arrives on Christmas Eve, one week after Avengers: Doomsday and Dune: Part Three open on December 18, making it the biggest Hindi release of the holiday season.

A Family First

King marks Shah Rukh Khan's first on-screen collaboration with his daughter Suhana Khan, who makes her theatrical debut in the film. The ensemble also includes Deepika Padukone, Abhishek Bachchan, and Rani Mukerji in key roles. The film is produced by Gauri Khan's Red Chillies Entertainment with Siddharth and Mamta Anand's Marflix Pictures, and Phars Film has acquired the overseas distribution rights.

The promotional campaign opened on November 2 — Shah Rukh Khan's birthday — with the release of the film's first song, Tu Mil Jaaye, featuring Khan and Deepika Padukone. The same month, Khan confirmed with a new poster that the film is not postponed and will release as scheduled on December 24.

The Business Before the Release

Even before a single ticket has been sold, King is already one of the biggest commercial stories of the year. Trade reports put its pre-release deals — domestic and overseas theatrical rights, digital streaming, satellite, and music — at around ₹665 crore, with the overseas deal alone reported as a record minimum-guarantee arrangement. Trade reporting also suggests the film would need a global haul of around ₹800 crore to break even. These are industry estimates reported by outlets such as Sacnilk and Pinkvilla, not audited studio figures — but the scale of the numbers shows how much is riding on the Christmas opening.

Trade reports put the production budget at around ₹350 crore, which would make King one of the most expensive Hindi films ever made.

Why This Comeback Carries Weight

The last time Shah Rukh Khan and Siddharth Anand worked together, Pathaan (2023) relaunched Khan's box-office run after a four-year gap from leading roles. King is the test of whether that comeback has a second act: a new director-actor pairing is not the story this time — the reunion is — and the film has to prove itself against two Hollywood tentpoles opening a week earlier, in the most crowded corridor of the year.

For Khan, the stakes are personal as well as commercial. Sharing the screen with his daughter for the first time, in the Christmas slot his biggest hits have owned, King is built to be an event. Whether it becomes one will be decided in its first weekend.

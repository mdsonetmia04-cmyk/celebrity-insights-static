I'm A Celebrity 2026: Wayne Rooney Leads 11 Campmates as the Jungle Returns Tonight

Trending
Nov 16, 2026
3 min read

I'm A Celebrity 2026 launches with Wayne Rooney leading 11 campmates. Lineup, start time, and how to watch the jungle series.

Advertisement
Advertisement

Britain's biggest reality show is back tonight. The 26th series of I'm A Celebrity... Get Me Out Of Here! launches on Monday, November 16, at 9pm on ITV1 and ITVX, with Ant and Dec presenting live from the Australian jungle. Eleven celebrities are heading into camp, led by former England and Manchester United captain Wayne Rooney — and this year the show makes history before a single trial begins.

Advertisement

The Line-Up

ITV confirmed the full line-up on November 11. The 11 campmates are: Wayne Rooney, former Everton, Manchester United and England striker turned manager and pundit; Joanna Page, who plays Stacey in Gavin & Stacey; Kaiser Chiefs frontman Ricky Wilson; Big Brother presenter and former Strictly contestant AJ Odudu; Paralympian and presenter Ade Adepitan; model Leomie Anderson; Love Island finalist and podcast host Chloe Burrows; broadcaster and author Mariella Frostrup; YouTuber Sharky, a member of the Beta Squad collective with 12 million subscribers; stand-up comedian Jack Skipper; and actress Claire Sweeney, known for Coronation Street, Brookside and musical theatre.

Advertisement

Rooney was the first star to touch down in Australia, following in the footsteps of his wife Coleen, who finished as runner-up in 2024. The 41-year-old arrives in the opening episode by helicopter and must jump out to join his fellow campmates — first-look footage shows him visibly nervous before the leap. He has admitted he will miss a Chinese takeaway the most.

Advertisement

A First for the Show

Ade Adepitan is the first wheelchair user to take part in the show's history. Producers spent months working with him on accessibility changes at the camp near Murwillumbah in New South Wales, including deeper pathways, wheelchair parking bays, rest areas, a stairlift to the jungle shower, and multiple wheelchairs and crutch storage. The camp is the same Springbrook/Dungay site the show has used since 2003.

Advertisement

When and How to Watch

The launch episode runs for 95 minutes, finishing at 10.35pm, and the companion show Unpacked follows each night on ITV2. The Monday start is a departure from tradition: the series usually launches on a Sunday, but ITV moved it because live coverage of the UEFA Nations League match between Spain and England occupied the Sunday slot.

Advertisement

In the opening episode, five celebrities — Page, Wilson, Sharky, Sweeney and Anderson — take on a challenge to win the camp's first meals. And late arrivals are a tradition on the show, so more famous faces could still be sent into the jungle once the series is under way.

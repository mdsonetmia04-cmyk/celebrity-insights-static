Suriya's Scene Crosses ₹250 Crore — His Third ₹200 Crore Film of 2026

Trending
Nov 18, 2026
2 min read

Suriya's Scene crossed ₹250 crore worldwide — his third ₹200 crore film of 2026. Box office figures, the record, and the unconfirmed Netflix date.

Suriya's crime thriller Scene has crossed ₹250 crore worldwide, collecting ₹2.90 crore net in India on Day 12 to take its worldwide gross to ₹252.45 crore, according to trade tracker Sacnilk. The film's India net total stands at ₹142.40 crore, with an India gross of ₹165.45 crore. All box-office figures are trade estimates.

The milestone seals a remarkable year: Suriya has become the first Tamil actor to have three films gross over ₹200 crore in the same calendar year. Directed by Jithu Madhavan, Scene opened in cinemas on November 6 in Tamil and Telugu, co-starring Naslen and Nazriya.

The Record-Breaking Year

Scene is the third leg of a hat-trick that began with Karuppu in May, which collected around ₹310 crore worldwide, and continued with Vishwanath & Sons in August, at around ₹209 crore. Scene passed the ₹200 crore mark on Day 7 and has since outperformed both earlier films in Kerala, setting a new career-best for Suriya in the state.

The feat puts Suriya in an elite Indian-cinema club previously occupied by Shah Rukh Khan, who did it in 2023, and Akshay Kumar, who did it in 2019. Scene is now chasing Karuppu's total to become the biggest film of Suriya's career. In the film, Suriya plays a police officer investigating a murder that resembles a "Perfume Killer" case he once solved.

The Netflix Question

Reports say Netflix has acquired Scene's digital rights for a reported ₹85–88 crore, with a four-week theatrical window that would point to a streaming debut around December 4. Neither Netflix nor the makers have officially confirmed the platform or the date, so early December remains a reported possibility rather than a confirmed launch.

The streaming premiere could also shift depending on how the film continues to perform in cinemas, where it is still playing in its third week.

Quick Answers

How much has Scene earned worldwide? ₹252.45 crore in 12 days, per Sacnilk. All figures are trade estimates.

What record has Suriya set? He is the first Tamil actor with three films grossing over ₹200 crore in the same calendar year: Karuppu, Vishwanath & Sons and Scene.

When does Scene come to Netflix? No official date has been announced. Reports point to around December 4 under a four-week window, but that is unconfirmed.

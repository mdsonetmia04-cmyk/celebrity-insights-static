Brad Pitt Is Back as Cliff Booth — The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth Explained

Hollywood
Nov 15, 2026
3 min read

Brad Pitt returns as Cliff Booth in The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth — IMAX Nov 25, 2026, then Netflix Dec 23. Plot and cast details.

Brad Pitt returns as Cliff Booth in The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth, which opens exclusively in IMAX theaters on Wednesday, November 25, 2026, for a two-week engagement before streaming on Netflix from December 23. It is a standalone sequel to Quentin Tarantino's Once Upon a Time in Hollywood (2019), the film that won Pitt the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor — and this time the pairing behind the camera is the story as much as the one in front of it: Tarantino wrote the screenplay, and David Fincher directs.

The Story: 1977, and a Different Job

The film is set in 1977 Los Angeles, eight years after the events of Once Upon a Time in Hollywood. Cliff Booth is no longer working as a stuntman. In the film's setup, the former stunt double has become a Hollywood fixer — the person called when the seedier corners of the industry need a problem handled quietly — while also working as a bartender and a projectionist.

That shift is the point. Fincher has described the film as less a stunt story or a movie-about-movies than an LA story, set in a Hollywood where the dream of the late 1960s has curdled into something harder. Netflix is keeping the full plot under wraps beyond the fixer setup, so anything past that outline remains unconfirmed — worth stating plainly, because speculation about the story has already run ahead of the facts.

Advertisement

Who Made It, and Why That Matters

Tarantino wrote the script but decided against directing it himself. He later said he did not want to disappoint Pitt by abandoning the project altogether, and that Fincher was the only other director he was comfortable handing it to. Fincher, who works exclusively with Netflix under a multi-picture deal, stepped in — making this his fourth collaboration with Pitt after Se7en (1995), Fight Club (1999), and The Curious Case of Benjamin Button (2008).

The film is also a first for Netflix: its first feature released exclusively in IMAX, and the company has said it will report the grosses from the two-week run — another unusual step for a streamer. The reported budget is $200 million. Trent Reznor and Atticus Ross composed the score, their sixth consecutive feature with Fincher. Netflix released a second official trailer on November 6, 2026.

The Cast

Alongside Pitt, the film stars Elizabeth Debicki, Scott Caan, Carla Gugino, and Yahya Abdul-Mateen II in central roles, with Peter Weller as Gus, Cliff's father. Timothy Olyphant returns from the 2019 film as Western actor James Stacy. The producers are Tarantino, Ceán Chaffin, David Heyman, Stacey Sher, and Pitt.

How to Watch

The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth opens in IMAX theaters worldwide on November 25, 2026, for two weeks only — there is no standard theatrical release planned beyond the IMAX engagement. It then premieres on Netflix on December 23, 2026, two days before Christmas. For viewers deciding between the two, the IMAX run is the only way to see it on a big screen, and the window is short.

Avril Lavigne Secretly Welcomes Her First Child — What She Shared

Trending

Avril Lavigne secretly welcomed her first child, a baby girl. What the singer shared on Instagram and what is known so far.

Avril Lavigne is a mother. The singer announced on Friday, November 13, 2026 that she has welcomed her first child, a baby girl, sharing the news herself on Instagram in a series of black-and-white photographs — with no advance announcement that she was expecting.

The Announcement

Lavigne, 42, posted three photographs: one of her and her partner holding the newborn's tiny hand, one of her holding the baby close to her chest, and one of a skateboard carrying two adult pairs of checkered Vans beside a miniature baby pair — a nod to the pop-punk image that made her famous with "Sk8er Boi" and "Complicated" more than two decades ago.

"Our Mini F***ing Princess has arrived," she captioned the post, adding ribbon, crown, bottle and heart emojis. Comments on the post were turned off.

What She Did Not Share

Lavigne has not revealed her daughter's name or date of birth, and she has not publicly identified her partner. A representative told People magazine that she has been enjoying time with her family while working on a new album, and thanked fans for their love.

The surprise is part of the story: unlike most celebrity pregnancy announcements, there was no build-up — no photos, no statements, no confirmation beforehand. Lavigne had kept a low profile for months, and the first the public knew of the pregnancy was the arrival itself.

A New Chapter, On Her Terms

For an artist whose career began as a teenager and has been lived almost entirely in public, the announcement was strikingly private in its details: the baby's face is not shown, no names are given, and the focus stays on the moment rather than the story around it. Fans will have to wait for whatever she chooses to share next — and on the evidence of this announcement, that will be when she decides, not before.

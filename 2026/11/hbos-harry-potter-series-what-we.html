HBO's Harry Potter Series: What We Actually Know So Far

Hollywood
Nov 16, 2026
3 min read

HBO's Harry Potter series premieres Dec 25, 2026. Season 1 cast, episodes, trailer details, and what is confirmed vs rumour.

HBO's Harry Potter series now has a premiere date, a full cast, and a trailer — and it is arriving sooner than the original plan suggested. Season 1, titled Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone, premieres on HBO in the United States and on HBO Max internationally on Friday, December 25, 2026. The first season runs eight episodes and adapts the first book, with one book per season planned across the series.

The New Trio

The three lead roles went to newcomers found through an open casting call that considered tens of thousands of young actors. Dominic McLaughlin plays Harry Potter, Arabella Stanton plays Hermione Granger, and Alastair Stout plays Ron Weasley. The trio was announced in May 2025, and filming began that July at Warner Bros. Studios Leavesden in Hertfordshire, England — the same studio complex where the original films were made.

The casting news has kept coming even as filming continued. In late October 2026, Jasper Talbot and Hannah Chinn were cast as James and Lily Potter, and Harry Gilby joined as Charlie Weasley.

Advertisement

The Adults Around Them

The adult cast is led by John Lithgow as Albus Dumbledore, Janet McTeer as Minerva McGonagall, Paapa Essiedu as Severus Snape, and Nick Frost as Rubeus Hagrid. The wider ensemble includes Lox Pratt as Draco Malfoy, Johnny Flynn as Lucius Malfoy, Katherine Parkinson as Molly Weasley, Bel Powley and Daniel Rigby as Petunia and Vernon Dursley, Luke Thallon as Quirinus Quirrell, and Bertie Carvel as Cornelius Fudge. Warwick Davis returns as Filius Flitwick — the first actor from the films to reprise a role in the series.

Francesca Gardiner, known for her Emmy-winning work on Succession, is the showrunner, writer, and an executive producer. Mark Mylod directs multiple episodes.

What Is Confirmed, and What Is Still Rumour

Confirmed: the Christmas Day 2026 premiere, the eight-episode first season, the lead and adult cast above, and a second season adapting Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets, which HBO renewed before the first season even aired. A first teaser arrived on March 25, 2026, followed by a fuller trailer on September 2.

Still unconfirmed or reported rather than announced: HBO has not published start times or a release schedule for episodes two through eight, so there is no confirmed finale date yet. Season 2 casting reports — including Kit Harington as Gilderoy Lockhart — come from trade reporting, not from an HBO announcement, and there is no Season 2 release date.

Why the Date Matters

When filming began in July 2025, the plan pointed to a 2027 debut. Moving the premiere to Christmas Day 2026 puts the biggest television bet of the decade into the year's most-watched week — and with a decade-long, book-by-book plan behind it, the first season has to land. The trailer numbers and the casting will tell HBO quickly whether it has.

The Thomas Crown Affair Trailer: Michael B. Jordan's Billionaire Art Thief Arrives March 5

Hollywood
Nov 17, 2026
2 min read

The Thomas Crown Affair trailer is out. Michael B. Jordan directs and stars as the billionaire art thief — in theatres March 5, 2027.

Amazon MGM Studios has released the full official trailer for The Thomas Crown Affair, offering the longest look yet at Michael B. Jordan's reimagining of the classic heist story. The trailer premiered on TODAY on Thursday, November 12, ahead of the film's theatrical release on March 5, 2027.

Jordan directs, produces and stars as Thomas Crown, a billionaire art thief whose carefully planned robberies put him on a collision course with an investigator played by Adria Arjona. Kenneth Branagh, Lily Gladstone, Danai Gurira and Pilou Asbæk co-star, with a score by Jon Batiste. The film opens in theatres and IMAX worldwide, with no streaming date announced yet.

Cat and Mouse Over Dinner

The two-and-a-half-minute trailer leans into glamour and tension. It opens with Crown and Arjona's investigator, Bennet, sizing each other up over dinner at a smart restaurant — a psychological duel that sets up the film's central chase over a stolen work of art. The footage keeps the stylish costumes, luxurious settings and mind games of the earlier films while raising the share of action.

Advertisement

The Third Thomas Crown

This is the third big-screen version of the story, after Steve McQueen's 1968 original and Pierce Brosnan's 1999 remake. A teaser arrived in July; the new trailer is the fullest look so far at Jordan's take, and his second feature as a director.

Advertisement

The wait is still a long one — nearly four months from trailer to opening night. But Amazon MGM is positioning the film as a theatrical event, with an IMAX release and no streaming date in sight, betting that the old-fashioned movie-star heist still has pull. The full trailer is available now on the studio's official YouTube channel.

Quick Answers

When does The Thomas Crown Affair release? March 5, 2027, in theatres and IMAX worldwide.

Who stars in The Thomas Crown Affair? Michael B. Jordan directs, produces and stars as Thomas Crown, with Adria Arjona as the investigator.

Where can I watch the trailer? The full trailer is available on Amazon MGM Studios' official YouTube channel.

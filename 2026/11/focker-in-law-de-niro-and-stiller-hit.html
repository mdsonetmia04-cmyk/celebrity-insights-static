Focker-in-Law: De Niro and Stiller Hit Berlin as Thanksgiving Release Nears

Hollywood
Nov 18, 2026
2 min read

Focker-in-Law hits US cinemas Nov 25, 2026. De Niro and Stiller's Berlin photocall, Ariana Grande's role, cast, and release details.

Robert De Niro and Ben Stiller posed for photographers in front of the Berlin TV Tower on November 17, ahead of the film's Germany premiere at the Zoo Palast that evening. The fourth film in the Meet the Parents franchise opens in US cinemas on Wednesday, November 25, and in German cinemas on November 26.

The Berlin stop is the latest on a tour that has already included the film's world premiere in New York last week. In Germany the film carries the title Meine Frau, unsere Schwiegertochter und ich.

The Story and the New Arrival

Focker-in-Law flips the original premise: Greg Focker's grown-up son Henry wants to get married, putting Greg in the position Jack Byrnes once occupied. Ariana Grande joins the cast as Olivia, the prospective daughter-in-law — a former FBI negotiation specialist whose background fuels the new comic tension.

Advertisement

Stiller returns as Greg and De Niro as Jack, with Owen Wilson, Teri Polo and Blythe Danner also back. New faces include Skyler Gisondo, Beanie Feldstein and Eduardo Franco. The film is written and directed by John Hamburg, who co-wrote the three previous Meet the Parents films.

A Franchise 25 Years On

The first Meet the Parents opened in 2000, making this reunion 25 years in the making. De Niro and Stiller are also producers on the new film, alongside Jane Rosenthal for Tribeca Productions and John Lesher for Red Hour Films.

Advertisement

The Thanksgiving release puts the comedy into one of the year's busiest cinema weeks, opening the same day as Disney's animated Hexed.

Quick Answers

When does Focker-in-Law come out? November 25, 2026, in US cinemas, and November 26 in Germany.

Who does Ariana Grande play? Olivia, the prospective daughter-in-law, a former FBI negotiation specialist.

Who directs Focker-in-Law? John Hamburg, who co-wrote the three previous Meet the Parents films.

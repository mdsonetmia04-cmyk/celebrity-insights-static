Robert Pattinson Is the New James Bond — Everything We Know About Bond 26

Hollywood
Nov 17, 2026
3 min read

Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond. Everything we know about Bond 26 — director Villeneuve, writer, and the 2028 plan.

Robert Pattinson is the new James Bond. Amazon MGM Studios announced the casting on Monday, November 16, 2026, ending years of speculation about who would succeed Daniel Craig as 007. The 40-year-old British actor becomes the seventh actor to play Bond in the official film series, and the first since Amazon MGM took over the franchise.

The announcement came through the official James Bond accounts, with a first image of Pattinson in a tuxedo, gun in hand. In a statement reported by Variety, Pattinson said that "to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime."

Who Pattinson Follows — and Who Was in the Running

Pattinson succeeds Daniel Craig, who ended his five-film run as Bond with No Time to Die in 2021. Before Craig, the role belonged to Sean Connery, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton and Pierce Brosnan — Pattinson is the seventh name on that list.

Speculation about Craig's replacement had run for years, and the BBC reported that actors including Josh O'Connor, Callum Turner and Jack Lowden were thought to have made it to the final screen tests. Pattinson's name had been in the conversation for even longer: in a 2012 interview he said he wanted to play Bond, but not for another 20 years. Fourteen years later, Amazon named him 007 at 40.

The Team Behind Bond 26

The next film will be the 26th official Bond film, directed by Denis Villeneuve — the filmmaker behind Dune and Arrival — from a screenplay by Steven Knight, the creator of Peaky Blinders. It will be the first Bond film developed under Amazon MGM Studios.

Pattinson's own career makes the casting easy to explain. He broke out in the Twilight series, rebuilt himself as a serious actor in independent films, and returned to blockbusters as Batman in The Batman — a role that proved he could carry a franchise built on a single iconic character. He also stars in Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey, which reaches digital platforms this week.

What Is Not Announced Yet

A title and a release date have not been announced. Reports, including from the BBC, say the film is expected to go into production in 2027 and arrive in 2028, but those dates have not been confirmed by the studio, and no other casting has been announced.

That leaves the biggest questions open: who plays the villain, who plays M, Q and Moneypenny, and what kind of Bond Villeneuve and Knight want to make. For now, the news itself is the story — after the longest gap in the official series, 007 finally has a face again.

Why the Casting Matters

Bond is the rare franchise where the casting announcement is itself a global news event. Pattinson is not the safe, familiar choice the series has often made — he is a former teen idol turned art-house actor turned Batman — and that is exactly why the announcement has travelled so far. Whether he makes the role his own will take years to judge. The first verdict, though, is already in: nobody is ignoring Bond 26.
